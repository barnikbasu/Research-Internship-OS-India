# Research Internship OS India

## 🇮🇳 Made in India. Built for Researchers.

An AI-powered platform designed to transform how Indian students discover, apply for, and succeed in research internships across top institutes like IITs, IISc, IISERs, IIITs, NITs, ISI, TIFR, DRDO labs, and more.

---

# 🚀 Vision

Research Internship OS India is not just another internship listing website.

It is a complete AI-first ecosystem that helps students:

- Discover hidden research opportunities
- Find professors intelligently
- Generate personalized cold emails
- Build research profiles
- Analyze professor research papers
- Improve selection chances
- Track applications professionally
- Prepare for research careers

The goal is to become:

> “India’s Default Platform for Research Internships”
